Find a tool for the task you want to complete. Your MCP client calls these tools during your conversation.

Detailed guides are available for **list_courses**, **get_course**, **search_course**, **search_courses** and **read_resource**. The other tools currently have short summaries.

## Courses and search

<div className="tool-catalog catalog-courses" data-locale="en">

- [`list_courses`](/reference/tools/list-courses/)

  List enrolled courses, with optional year and semester filters.

- [`get_course`](/reference/tools/get-course/)

  Browse sections and discovered resource links.

- [`search_course`](/reference/tools/search-course/)

  Search section and activity text in one course.

- [`search_courses`](/reference/tools/search-courses/)

  Search across explicitly selected courses and resources.

</div>

## Materials

<div className="tool-catalog catalog-materials" data-locale="en">

- [`read_resource`](/reference/tools/read-resource/)

  Read supported Moodle pages and text-based PDFs.

- `get_folder`

  Discover files in a course folder.

- `read_folder_file`

  Read a discovered PDF from a folder.

</div>

## Coursework and announcements

<div className="tool-catalog catalog-coursework" data-locale="en">

- `get_assignment`

  Read an assignment and its published dates.

- `get_quiz`

  Read quiz information and published dates.

- `get_coursework_deadlines`

  Find published coursework dates across selected courses.

- `get_announcements`

  Read supported course news posts.

</div>

## Labs and preferences

<div className="tool-catalog catalog-labs" data-locale="en">

- `get_lab_timetable`

  Read published timetable evidence.

- `get_lab_schedule`

  Find dated lab candidates for a course and week.

- `get_weekly_labs`

  Look up labs for an explicitly selected week.

- `get_choice_selection`

  Read your saved Moodle poll selection.

- `get_lab_preference`

  Read your local attendance preference.

- `set_lab_preference`

  Save or reset a local attendance preference.

- `get_lab_term_preference`

  Read your local group and term preference.

- `set_lab_term_preference`

  Save or reset local group and term preferences.

</div>

## Local cache

<div className="tool-catalog catalog-cache" data-locale="en">

- `get_course_cache`

  Inspect local course cache information.

- `clear_course_cache`

  Remove the selected course's local cache.

</div>

## Access and results

Moodle access is read-only. Preference setters and cache clearing change local data. Check source links, coverage and warnings before relying on a result.
