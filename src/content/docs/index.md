## What is courses-mcp?

courses-mcp connects course materials to an MCP client so you can find and read course information from your chat.

## What can I use it for?

Ask your MCP client to find course information or help you locate relevant materials.

## Where can I follow the project?

Follow development in the [courses-mcp repository](https://github.com/mahmutmft/courses-mcp).
